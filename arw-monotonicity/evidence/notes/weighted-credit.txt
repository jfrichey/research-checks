3. GENERAL CREATION-LAW CHANGE OF MEASURE (PROOF)

Write t(x)=1-h(x), t(v)=0 and t(sink)=1. First assume t(x)>0 at every
non-target vertex. Define, for x!=v,

 K^t(x,y)=K(x,y)t(y)/t(x),                         (2)

including the sink. Since t is harmonic off v and the sink, these rows sum
to one, and the transformed kernel has no jump into v. It still has an
accessible sink: any positive-probability original sink path avoiding v
has positive transformed probability. KEEP p_x UNCHANGED.

For a finite configuration c, let rho(c) be the product of t(x) over all
particles, counting multiplicity and ignoring activity/sleep status. A
sleep leaves rho unchanged. A jump x->y changes it by t(y)/t(x), including
waking and sink loss. Therefore every finite legal history avoiding v
satisfies the telescoping likelihood identity

 P^t(history)=P(history) rho(final)/rho(initial).  (3)

The same identity holds for any prescribed causal legal scheduler used
in both systems. It also holds with ineffective sleep self-loops, or after
discarding them. For stopped stabilization histories, sum (3) over their
countable disjoint realizations; finite ARW stabilization is almost sure.

Apply this to DF's creator: stabilize active A\{z} while avoiding v and z,
ending at sleeping support B. Let nu be its original SUBPROBABILITY law,
and let nu^t be the corresponding creator law under (2), with the SAME
sleep probabilities, avoiding z. Then

 rho(B) nu(B) = rho(A\{z}) nu^t(B).                (4)

This holds at every particle bound, not just two. It preserves the
generating law; no arbitrary sleeping input distribution is substituted.
Neither side is divided by a success probability.

IMPORTANT SCOPE: (2) conditions the extended jump paths to avoid v. It is
not the ordinary ARW law conditioned on the event that stabilization never
visits v. That latter conditioning would generally change sleep decisions.
Equation (4), with its endpoint weight, is the precise assertion used here.

If some t(x)=0, division in (2) is not defined. Add positive direct sink
leakage, prove the identities for that regularized kernel, then consider
limits of cross-multiplied identities. Such a limit need not provide a
nondegenerate tilted representation at the original zero-t states. This
remains an explicit issue for using the transformation in a general proof;
it is not a restriction added to the occupation conjecture.

4. THE UNIFORM CONSTANT IS A HARMONIC PROJECTION (PROOF)

For a target-free binary support A, put rho(A)=product_(x in A)t(x), with
rho(empty)=1. For every z in A,

 J_z rho(A)=rho(A).                              (5)

Indeed, hold A\{z} fixed and follow the released particle until a vacancy
or the sink. Its t-value is a bounded martingale. Multiplying by the fixed
particles' t-values gives (5), with target-hit exits assigned value zero.
This does not require t>0; zeros cause no division here.

Let R(A,.)=sum_(z in A)(delta_A-J_z(A,.)), the uniform forced-jump defect
matrix. In the saved uniform decomposition

 ell = c0 delta_empty + U R,

multiply by rho and use (5). The result is

 c0 = sum_A ell(A)rho(A).                         (6)

This identifies the constant at every finite particle bound where the
response row ell is defined. It does NOT establish c0>=0 because ell can
have either sign. Equation (5) also shows the harmonic transform of J_z
is exactly the forced-vacancy walk for K^t; it introduces no new routing
rule. The earlier AF/CR source sections were checked before using R.

5. WHAT THE TRANSFORM DOES TO THE SIGNED CREATION COEFFICIENTS

Define U^t(A)=U(A)rho(A). By (4), the creation coefficients satisfy

 kappa^t(B,z)=rho(B)t(z) kappa(B,z),               (7)

where kappa^t is built from U^t, nu^t and the SAME odds p_z/(1-p_z).
For the no-contact kernel Q_B(x,y)=(1-p_x)K(x,y),

 (I-Q_B^t)^(-1)(x,y)
       = (I-Q_B)^(-1)(x,y) t(y)/t(x).

Consequently the resummed weights obey

 w_B^t(y)=rho(B)t(y)w_B(y).                       (8)

At common bound two, collision at y has B={y}, so its transformed
coefficient is d_y^t=t(y)^2 d_y. These are algebraic identities, not a claim
that U^t is the occupation-response row of a new target problem. The
transform preserves every sign when t>0. It cannot by itself fix F10 or
prove a positive budget. Any proof must compare different histories or
combine direct credit with negative mass after this normalization.

One further useful exact identity follows by summing (3) over all terminal
supports S for two initial actives at y:

 G_y=1-t(y)^2 E^t_y[1/rho(S)].                    (9)

Hence the ceiling gap is
 u_y-G_y=t(y)^2(E^t_y[1/rho(S)]-1)>=0.
This records exactly what the hitting ceiling discards: terminal sleeping
mass under a genuine ARW law. It is a structural identity, not an independent
proof of the budget, and using the computed expectation as an oracle would
merely recover G_y.
