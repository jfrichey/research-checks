2. AVOIDANCE NORMALIZATION REMOVES THE CREDIT HISTORY SPACE

Let t=1-h and rho(c)=product_i t(x_i), ignoring activity labels and counting
particle multiplicity. WC's transform K^t(x,y)=K(x,y)t(y)/t(x), where
t(x)>0, retains sleep probabilities and satisfies

 P^t(history)=P(history) rho(final)/rho(initial)

for target-avoiding finite histories. In particular K^t(x,v)=0. Every
history that first visits the target has probability ZERO under P^t.

Thus direct target-credit histories cannot themselves form a positive
donor submeasure of this avoidance-transformed law. An argument can retain
their original boundary flux separately, but then it uses two measures or
additional marked data. It cannot obtain that flux merely by revealing
more of an ordinary K^t-ARW history. This identifies a missing element in
the preceding proposed pairing; it does not refute pairings with separately
tracked boundary credit.

3. THE COMPLEMENTARY SUCCESS TRANSFORM (GENERAL PROOF)

Put F(c)=1-rho(c). Before a target visit, F is harmonic for every legal
instruction: sleep preserves positions and F; a jump is harmonic in the
chosen particle's coordinate. Sink loss is included by t(sink)=1.
Consequently, at configurations with F(c)>0, the complementary Doob kernel
on configurations is

 P^+(c,c')=P(c,c') F(c')/F(c).                    (1)

Stop at the first target visit, giving that boundary F=1, and freeze stable
states if reached first. Rows in (1) sum to one. Sleep probabilities remain
unchanged, including ineffective sleeps at collisions. A finite history
has the likelihood ratio F(final)/F(initial). Summing stopped target-hit
histories yields

 H_v(c)=F(c) P^+_c(target hit before stabilization). (2)

For a collision at y, F(2 delta_y)=u_y. Thus the exact collision term is
d_y u_y times a genuine probability under P^+. This represents the target
credit in a positive probability space; it does not prove any inequality
among its signed coefficients.

Unlike the avoidance transform, P^+ is generally NOT ordinary ARW with a
single altered site jump kernel. The factor F(c')/F(c) depends on the other
particles. Assuming closure in ordinary ARW would lose precisely this
dependence.

4. MINIMAL EXACT OBSTRUCTION TO A FIXED SUCCESS KERNEL

Vertices are 0 and target v=1, plus sink. Let 0 jump to v or sink with equal
probability and v jump to sink. Take p_0=2/3, p_v=1/2; all assumptions hold.
Then h(0)=1/2, F(delta_0)=1/2 and F(2 delta_0)=3/4.

Under (1), CONDITIONAL ON A JUMP:

 - with one particle at 0, target probability is 1 and sink probability 0;
 - with two particles at 0, target probability is 2/3 and sink probability 1/3.

Both jumps originate at the same site, so no site-only jump kernel can
represent both. The two-active original target-hit probability is 7/12;
under P^+ it is 7/9, and (3/4)(7/9)=7/12, verifying (2).

This is F14, scoped to a SINGLE ordinary-ARW normalization for success
credit. It is not an occupation counterexample or a failure of the

5. EXACT COLOURED-PATH REPRESENTATION

Use labelled particles and independent infinite jump chains for their
unread jump trajectories, stopped at the sink. Sleep instructions remain
independent with the original probabilities. For each particle i, mark
whether its entire extended jump chain will visit v. Given positions x_i,
the marks are independent Bernoulli(h(x_i)) before conditioning. Condition
on at least one success mark. A nonempty successful set J has probability

 [product_(i in J) h(x_i) product_(i notin J)t(x_i)] / F(c). (3)

Given these colours, use K^h(x,y)=K(x,y)h(y)/h(x) for a successful particle
until its first target visit, and K^t for an avoiding particle. Keep the
same sleep probabilities. In particular a success-coloured particle may
fall asleep forever before using the part of its extended path that would
visit v. This is NOT conditioning on an actual ARW target visit.

Run any fixed causal COLOUR-BLIND legal scheduler. Before the first target
visit, the posterior colour law, conditional on the full revealed history,
is exactly (3) evaluated at CURRENT particle positions. To prove this,
multiply its initial weight by the colour-specific path likelihood ratios.
For each particle the factors telescope from its initial h or t to its
current h or t; sleep instructions and colour-blind scheduling factors
cancel. Normalization is F(current). This retains the full creation history
and imposes no independence assumption on the positions produced by ARW.

Mixing the next jump over this posterior gives exactly (1). Thus the
coloured construction realizes the required configuration-dependent law
using fixed kernels for individual colours. Particles lost to the sink
have h=0 and can only carry the avoiding colour. Zero-probability colour
choices are never assigned; no division at such choices is required.

6. CONTACT EXCHANGE LEMMA, AND ITS LIMIT

At a colour-blind stopping time before a target visit, suppose two labelled
particles occupy the same vertex y. Their posterior colour law in (3) is
symmetric under exchanging their labels. Given the colours and revealed
history, their unused jump chains are independent chains of the appropriate
colours, both starting at y. Therefore the joint conditional law of

 (colour of particle i, unused chain i;
  colour of particle j, unused chain j)

is invariant under exchanging the two pairs. The same holds when one was
sleeping immediately before contact: both are awake after the arrival, and
the prior creation history remains part of the conditioning. Future sleep
instructions remain untouched and independent. This is an exact conditional
probability-preserving exchange of auxiliary colours and unused chains.

It is only a local conditional-law lemma. It does not by itself change the
unlabelled configuration, supply extra credit, or construct a bijection
between different already-consumed histories. In particular it does not
resolve F3's inventory obstruction or F1's arbitrary-sleeper comparison.
Nor should a colour-dependent choice of revelation time or scheduler be
inserted while retaining the colour-blind posterior formula.

In the two-particle example, the possible success-colour pairs are
(success,avoid), (avoid,success), (success,success), each with probability
1/3. Moving the first label gives target probability 2/3, as required.
verify_success.py checks these fractions, the prefix likelihood ratio and
the target-hitting identity exactly, without any graph search.
