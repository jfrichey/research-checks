ARW MONOTONICITY: COMPLETE SLEEP BLOCKS AND A FINITE INVERSE
Continuation of CONTROLLED_ROUTING.txt, 2026-10-06 UTC

STATUS / WHAT CHANGED

The add-one occupation conjecture and the arbitrary-graph positive-flow
lemma remain open. This pass makes two concrete changes to the proof search.

1. Balancing each renewal generation separately is false, even with a
   homogeneous sleep probability on four vertices. Balancing every finite
   prefix of generations is also false under the proposed avoidance cone.
   An exact certificate below shows that later generations are essential.
   This is NOT a counterexample to the actual ARW occupation conjecture,
   nor to the full-resolvent positive-flow lemma.

2. There is a finite, graph-general replacement for unreading successive
   mixed boundaries: unread every sleeper in a completed configuration
   together. This gives an explicit signed inverse of the entire all-active
   stabilization matrix, built using only simultaneous initial jumps and
   IDLA. The derivation is given below, with independent rational audits.
   It avoids a renewal-series convergence assumption. Positivity of the
   marked comparison is still missing; this is an algebraic framework for
   whole-history cancellation, not the desired coupling already constructed.

The useful next object is a whole marked sleep block, with every initial
factor retained until its joint IDLA routing is resolved. Do not go back to
requiring positive weights at each individual first-contact renewal.

1. WHY RENEWAL GENERATIONS CANNOT BE BALANCED IN ISOLATION

Keep D=M D+G_v a_v from the preceding notes. A tempting sufficient lemma
would say that every row of M^k G_v is nonnegative on the cone

    f>=0,  J_z f(B)<=f(B),  z in B,

of functions on target-free common supports. If true, and with suitable
convergence, one could sum independently balanced generations. It is false.
Even the same assertion for sum_{j=0}^k M^j G_v is false.

Here is an exact four-vertex example. The sink is dagger and v=0:

    0 -> 1,2,dagger with probabilities 1/4,1/4,1/2;
    1 -> 2,3 with probabilities 1/2,1/2;
    2 -> 2,3 with probabilities 1/2,1/2;
    3 -> 0,3 with probabilities 1/2,1/2.

Every sleep probability is 1/2. Take common A={1,2}, extra x=3, and
construct the renewal on states with at most two common particles.
Define the artificial test function

    f({1})=1, f({2})=2,
    f(B)=0 for every other target-free support B.

This f is nonnegative and satisfies ALL the forced-jump inequalities.
For singleton supports the inequalities are the one-particle inequalities
u>=K u, with u(0)=u(3)=u(dagger)=0, u(1)=1, u(2)=2.
For supports of size at least two, a forced walk cannot reach the original
sink before hitting target 0. Killing on the target therefore leaves no
transition to a smaller nonempty support. Both sides of these inequalities
are zero. This explains the witness without relying on an LP tolerance.

For the specified response row, exact rational arithmetic gives

    (G_v f)_(A,x) = (M G_v f)_(A,x) = 0,

    (M^2 G_v f)_(A,x)
      = -83116240114 / 15938335654558317
      = -0.000005214863202496868...,

    (M^3 G_v f)_(A,x)
      = 17213694231404477 / 66941009749144931400 > 0.

Thus both generation two and the prefix through generation two are negative.
The fully solved response on the same test function is positive:

    ((I-M)^(-1) G_v f)_(A,x)
      = 201928285976789061 / 652530389578693808000
      = 0.0003094542249705244... .

The test function is NOT asserted to be an actual ARW avoidance function;
in particular f(empty)=0, whereas actual avoidance has a_v(empty)=1.
It legitimately refutes the proposed generationwise cone lemma, which was
required to hold on all nonnegative forced-jump-superharmonic vectors.
No conclusion about the sign of M^2 G_v at actual avoidance values follows.

This strengthens the architectural reason to retain complete histories:
some of the positive payment is in a later renewal, not merely elsewhere
within the current renewal. Truncating at a deterministic number of renewals
can discard exactly the compensating histories.

2. A FINITE INVERSE BUILT FROM COMPLETE SLEEP BLOCKS

Work on any finite vertex set V with an accessible sink, jump kernel K,
and 0<p_z<1. Write q_z=1-p_z. Let

    P(A,B) = P(final sleeping support is B | initial active support is A)

for all subsets A,B of V. These are binary ALL-ACTIVE inputs. P includes
the empty support and is a square stochastic matrix of size 2^|V|.

For S subset A define a stochastic kernel R_S(A,.) as follows:

  * Keep one active particle at each z in A\S.
  * For each z in S independently sample one destination with law K(z,.),
    and place that particle there, deleting it at the original sink.
  * From the resulting all-active configuration, move only surplus
    particles until every site has at most one particle (IDLA).

The selected initial particles all take their prescribed initial step
BEFORE any IDLA reduction. A selected site can be reoccupied by another
selected particle. Sink accessibility gives almost-sure finite reduction.
R_empty(A,.)=delta_A; R_{z}(A,.) is the old forced-jump kernel J_z.

Define the signed matrix

    E(A,.) = [prod_{z in A} p_z]^(-1)
             sum_{S subset A} (-1)^|S| [prod_{z in S} q_z] R_S(A,.).    (1)

Then, on every such finite graph,

    E P = P E = I.                                                    (2)

Thus the all-active stabilization matrix is always invertible and its
inverse has the finite formula (1). The formula is a consequence of the
Abelian property and first-instruction identities; no claim of literature
novelty is made here. It is useful for this research because it packages
unreading all sleeps in a single finite block.

3. PROOF OF THE INVERSE IDENTITY

Let a_z be the stochastic operator that adds one active particle at z to
a stable configuration and stabilizes. Set a_dagger=I. These operators
commute by the Abelian property. Let j_z=sum_y K(z,y)a_y.

Adding two active particles at z forces a jump before a sleep can be
effective, irrespective of whether the initial stable configuration has a
sleeper at z. Consequently

    a_z^2 = a_z j_z.                                                 (3)

Define the generally SIGNED operator

    b_z = (a_z-q_z j_z)/p_z.                                        (4)

If z is empty in the starting sleeping support B, conditioning the first
instruction of the newly added particle gives

    delta_B a_z = p_z delta_(B union {z}) + q_z delta_B j_z,

so delta_B b_z=delta_(B union {z}). Since the b_z commute, starting empty
and applying each b_z for z in A produces exactly delta_A:

    delta_empty prod_{z in A} b_z = delta_A.                         (5)

Expand the product in (5). The term indexed by S replaces the factors
a_z for z in S by j_z and has coefficient
(-1)^|S| prod_{z in S}q_z / prod_{z in A}p_z.
The positive polynomial prod_{z in A\S}a_z prod_{z in S}j_z describes
the simultaneous initial steps defining R_S. By (3), or directly by
legal surplus topplings, its full stabilization law is R_S(A,.) P.
Therefore (5) says E P=I, row by row. A square matrix with a left inverse
is invertible, proving P E=I as well.

No assumption about invertibility or spectral radius of the OLD signed
edge matrix I-M enters this argument. Conversely, this argument does NOT
prove that I-M itself is invertible on all of its formal edge coordinates.
E is a different matrix, on full support laws rather than response edges.

4. WHY THE SIMULTANEOUS BLOCK MATTERS

It would be incorrect to replace R_S by a product of ordinary successive
J_z moves. During a simultaneous selection, all selected particles' old
positions are released together; a sequential move keeps the other initial
particles in place. The two routing geometries differ.

For example, take the two-vertex path dagger-0-1-dagger with p=1/2.
From A={0,1}, R_{0,1} is uniform on empty,{0},{1},{0,1}. Formula (1) gives

    E(A,.) = (13/4)delta_{0,1} - (5/4)delta_0
             - (5/4)delta_1 + (1/4)delta_empty.                      (6)

In contrast, J_0 followed by J_1 gives masses 1/16 at {0,1}, 9/16 at
{0}, and 3/8 at empty. Simply multiplying attractive single-particle
updates therefore does not implement this inverse block.

Equation (6) also shows why E itself is not just an ordinary killed
Markov generator: there are positive off-diagonal entries. Its inverse
is stochastic because it is the actual stabilization matrix P, not because
of an unjustified M-matrix sign argument.

There is an exact collision identity, directly from (3)-(4):

    a_z b_z = a_z j_z.                                              (7)

On a valid empty-site sleep-creation history, the left side adds a sleeper
and then an active particle; the right side describes the forced jump
after they meet. This is a candidate bookkeeping rule for moving a mark
through a whole sleep block. It is an equality of laws, not yet an
inverse-recognizable map on sampled instruction fields. In particular b_z
must NOT be used as a stochastic transition on arbitrary stable inputs.

5. CONCRETE NEXT GENERAL PROOF TARGET

For v fixed, let h_v(B)=1{v in B}. The desired binary increment is

    (delta_(A union {x})-delta_A) P h_v,
    P=E^(-1), x outside A.                                         (8)

The preceding controlled-flow framework remains a sufficient way to prove
(8) nonnegative. The complete-block formula is a new way to organize the
history cancellation needed to CONSTRUCT that flow, or possibly to avoid
the stronger avoidance-cone lemma entirely.

Suggested architecture:

  (a) Keep a complete selected sleep/jump block and its marked extra input.
  (b) Resolve joint IDLA routes before treating a signed term as a debt.
  (c) At a collision use (7) to transfer the marked role, retaining the
      selected-site labels and all jump records required for reversal.
  (d) Pair opposite signed blocks, or construct a disjoint fractional
      balance, only after all returns belonging to the block are included.

The missing invariant is still substantive: prove the unmatched marked
mass at the target has nonnegative sign and preserves the intended
marginals. Formula (1) does not establish this automatically; (7) alone
does not establish it either. This is the next construction to attempt,
not an assertion that the general coupling now exists.

Binary support is sufficient for the original all-active problem once
the binary response inequalities are proved. First perform legal IDLA on
an arbitrary all-active input, holding one active particle per occupied
site. IDLA from one extra particle can be coupled so the binary supports
are equal or differ by one site, and unused stack suffixes are fresh.
Then apply the binary inequalities conditionally. This does not extend
the claim to arbitrary initially sleeping configurations.
