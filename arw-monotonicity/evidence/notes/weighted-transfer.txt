2. General weighted-transfer lemma (PROOF)
------------------------------------------
Consider any two complete-history laws with contact bit J and target bit I.
Assume 0<c_minus<c_plus<1, and set

 a=1-c_plus, b=c_plus-c_minus, c=c_minus.

Use nested flag-pair masses a,b,c for (0,0),(0,1),(1,1). Suppose

 D_0=u_0-l_0 >= 0, D_1=u_1-l_1 >= 0, delta=l_0-u_1 > 0.

The bad cross-pair demand is d=b*delta. The two ordered pairs have capacities
a*D_0 and c*D_1. If

             a*D_0+c*D_1 >= d,                         (B)

set e=min(d,a*D_0), t=d-e. Then 0<=t<=c*D_1. Replace the conditional target
parameters within the three flag pairs by

 pair     lower target parameter          upper target parameter
 (0,0)    l_0+e/a                         u_0
 (0,1)    l_0-e/b                         u_1+t/b
 (1,1)    l_1                             u_1-t/c.

Each pair is ordered; in (0,1) the parameters are equal because e+t=d.
All six parameters lie in [0,1]. For example l_0+e/a<=u_0<=1,
l_0-e/b>=u_1>=0, u_1+t/b<=l_0<=1, and u_1-t/c>=l_1>=0.
Couple the target bits using a shared uniform within each flag pair.

The lower success mass moved into (0,0) is e and the mass removed from
(0,1) is e. Both pairs have lower J=0. The upper success mass moved into
(0,1) is t and removed from (1,1) is t; both have upper J=1. Hence each
copy's full OWN (J,I) distribution is unchanged. Finish by sampling its
original complete history conditional on its own (J,I). Disintegration
preserves both complete-history laws, while target and contact indicators
are ordered. No stack-by-stack inverse or LP is required for this abstract
normalization lemma.

What changes relative to CE: a copy's target law may depend on the partner's
contact flag. It returns to the correct law after averaging over that flag.
This conditional tilt is necessary in the perturbed example. There is no
assumption of unchanged conditional path laws at the intermediate boundary.

----------------------------------------
In the example,

 d = 67066465230783/67204163676101900,
 a*D_0 = 2057706170811111/67204163676101900,
 c*D_1 = 30825490206269/10080624551415285.

The first capacity already exceeds d. Thus take e=d and t=0: shift only
lower success mass from flag pair (0,1) to (0,0). The new lower parameters
are

 (0,0): 20553023535910399/33602081838050950,
 (0,1): 407036482804361/672041636761019 = u_1,
 (1,1): l_1.

All upper parameters remain unchanged within their own flag class.
The exact implementation has 488 positive rational entries. It checks both
joint laws of (contact flag, full terminal support), unit mass, zero bad
target mass, and contact order. Full-history preservation follows from the
conditional sampling argument. The weighted five-configuration decomposition
is also checked against direct complete stabilization of (1,-1,1,0) and
(1,-1,1,-1) at these new sleep probabilities.

4. The remaining bottleneck is substantive, not normalization
-------------------------------------------------------------
There is an important limitation to the lemma. Direct expansion gives

 a*D_0+c*D_1-d
   = a*u_0+(b+c)*u_1 - (a+b)*l_0-c*l_1
   = P_plus(I=1)-P_minus(I=1) = G.

Thus (B) is EXACTLY the target inequality, once the other conditional signs
are assumed. It is not an independent proof of ARW monotonicity. In this
finite example we know G from exact absorption; using that sign to build a
coupling is legitimate finite construction but cannot bootstrap a general
proof. More fitting of contact transports would not resolve this bottleneck.

What this chunk establishes is (i) the earlier proposed cross-class
inequality is not structurally valid, even with the same corridor and an
explicit generating history; (ii) individual negative branches can be
repaired with exact normalization; (iii) the unresolved task is an
ARW-specific positive certificate for the TOTAL budget, permitting negative
intermediate contributions.

Next direction: park attempts to prove individual contact-conditioned
orders. Return to the checkpoint's positive avoidance-flow direction (AF),
starting with its complete-response identity only. Seek a telescoping or
forced-jump-defect certificate for the full budget after all renewal
responses are summed. Do not demand positivity of a generation or prefix
(F5), and do not present an abstract transport criterion as a proof of the
budget it assumes. Retain the contact constructions as a way to convert an
established positive budget into an explicitly normalized coupling.
