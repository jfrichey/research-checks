STATUS

The general all-active add-one occupation conjecture remains open. This
chunk disproves the specific no-contact coefficient invariant proposed in
DF, then repairs that example by carrying a block through a wake-up and a
two-particle move. Both the obstruction and repair are exact. They identify

Question: after DF's creation-history factorization of the FULL response,
are the weights w_B=kappa_B(I-Q_B)^(-1) always nonnegative? Q_B follows the
one active particle with sleeping background B until collision, sleep,
target hit or sink exit. Stopping criterion: a verified obstruction or a
useful general identity. The obstruction appeared in a short deterministic
rate sweep; no long-running job remains.

1. SOURCES, ASSUMPTIONS, AND WHAT CHANGED

Keep exactly the canonical assumptions: a finite graph, a K-accessible
absorbing sink from every vertex, 0<p_z<1, and all-active original inputs.
Only occupation of a specified target is compared.

DF Section 1 proves the general creation-history identity

 e(A,z)=a(A)-(J_z a)(A)
       =p_z/(1-p_z) sum_B nu_{A\{z}}^{v,z}(B) H_v(B,z).

Here a is target avoidance, J_z is forced jump plus surplus IDLA, nu is the
UNNORMALIZED law of creating sleeping B without visiting z or target v,
and H_v(B,z) is target hitting from active z and sleeping B.

Apply it to the full signed uniform-routing response from AF/CR:

 Delta=c0+sum_A U(A)sum_{z in A}e(A,z)
      =c0+sum_{B,z}kappa(B,z)H_v(B,z).

DF resums Q_B(z,y)=(1-p_z)K(z,y) on V\(B union {v}), obtaining

 Delta=c0+sum_B w_B g_B,       w_B=kappa_B(I-Q_B)^(-1).

g_B records target entry and the eventual target-hit credits of collision
states. It is nonnegative. The inverse is always well-defined because the
Q_B row sums are at most max_z(1-p_z)<1. The proposed sign of w_B is the
extra claim tested here. This is not AF's still-unproved general signed
renewal invertibility. All finite renewal systems used here are invertible.

F1-F9 were checked. This works on complete response rows, so it does not
repeat the generationwise positivity assertion in F5. It retains actual
creation laws and mixed ARW dynamics, rather than the unrestricted sleeping
attractiveness of F1 or the IDLA substitutions of F6. No new conditional
contact transport is fitted.

3. NEGATIVE MASS SURVIVES FIRST COLLISION

Collect target-entry credits into C and collision credits by the actual
resulting configuration. Because |B|<=1 here, every collision state has two
active particles at one site and no sleepers. Let G_i be its target-hit
probability. Then exactly

 Delta=C+d_0 G_0+d_2 G_2+d_3 G_3,
 C>0, d_0>0, d_3>0, d_2<0.

The negative coefficient is d_2=(3/4)w_{ {2} }(0). Thus simply grouping the
first-collision outcomes also fails. Exact values and all original response,
creation, kappa, w and collision coefficients are in exact_failure.json.

4. A BLOCK CONTINUING THROUGH THE WAKE-UP PAYS THE DEBT

Start with two actives at 0. Ignore ineffective sleep instructions until
the mandatory jump 0->2. The state is then one active at 0 and one at 2.
Hold the particle at 2 and process the particle at 0 once. It sleeps with
probability p_0=1/4, leaving sleeping 0 and active 2; otherwise it jumps
to 2, leaving two actives there. Fresh suffixes and a legal schedule give

 G_0=(1/4)H_1({0},2)+(3/4)G_2.                              (1)

Put b=-d_2>0 and alpha=b/(3/4). Then

 d_0 G_0-b G_2
   =(d_0-alpha)G_0+(alpha/4)H_1({0},2).                     (2)

The exact capacity check is

 (3/4)d_0+d_2
 =506030510093147230092338021523366553593081246900684678334762521 /
  684274512928081443474751492864307498156053267862262167925557611724800
 >0.

Consequently (2), together with C and d_3G_3, is a nonnegative complete
response certificate. All its coefficients and its equality with the raw
occupation increment are checked rationally. This is a local transition
identity with an explicit capacity check, not a fit using the desired
target inequality.

The mechanism changes the sleeping background: the debt arose from a
sleeper at 2, while the donor's alternative outcome has a sleeper at 0.
Keeping each background separate through the first collision misses this
exchange. A collision need not be a valid block boundary for coefficient
positivity; the woken particle must sometimes travel jointly with the
particle that woke it. Equation (1) makes that requirement concrete here.

More generally, for distinct z,y and a fixed sleeping background disjoint
from z,y,v, two actives at z can both move along z->y before any other particle
is toppled. This event has probability (1-p_z)K(z,y)^2, giving a legitimate
stopped-history lower bound by that weight times the two-active-at-y hit
probability. Other outcomes retain their nonnegative complete-history
credits. This elementary transition bound provides no general capacity
theorem: paying all debts is still the missing step.

5. NEXT CONCRETE QUESTION

Retire universal no-contact w_B positivity (new failure F10). The next
specific candidate is to resum the ENTIRE two-active phase at common
particle bound two: from the signed collision source d, use the raw ARW
kernel Q^(2) on configurations with exactly two active particles, choosing
the least-index active site as scheduler. Stop on the first
effective sleep, sink loss or target hit. Test whether the entrance/visit
weights d(I-Q^(2))^(-1) are nonnegative on saved heterogeneous cases and
the recorded rate grid. This allows the pair motion in (1).

A surviving negative weight is a scoped obstruction and should end that
test. If it survives, inspect the boundary creation laws before inventing
another arbitrary positivity cone. If it passes, seek a coefficient-balance
identity; finite success alone is not proof. At larger particle counts,
additional wakes introduce further active particles, so a two-active block
is only a diagnostic step toward full avalanche grouping. AF's general
renewal invertibility and total budget construction remain open.
