LOCAL WEIGHTED TRANSPORT OF COMPLETED ARW HISTORIES
2026-10-05, second continuation

Status: the general single-site add-one conjecture is still unproved here.
This continuation advances the repair architecture beyond arbitrary pairing
of bad and good atoms. It gives an exact coupling whose paired geometries are
connected by short, explicitly restricted stack rewrites; proves a general
parameter-extension lemma; and identifies two genuine obstructions to more
rigid repair rules. All computational conclusions below concern the specified
720-geometry reservoir, not arbitrary graphs or unrestricted couplings.

1. WHAT IS BEING COUPLED

Keep the previous notation: Xi is the strong geometry (ordinary outside
stacks and target jump directions, with target sleep gaps removed), L_minus
and L_plus are its chance counts, and G is an independent geometric rank with

    P(G=j) = p*z^(j-1),   z=1-p.

For marked cell (Xi,j), set f=1{j<=L_minus} and g=1{j<=L_plus}.
A bad cell has type 10; a good cell has type 01. Completed fields of types
00 and 11 may be useful intermediate search states. They are not sources or
destinations of bad-to-good mass in the final fractional pairing.

Use the same path sink-0-1-2-sink, eta=(1,0,1), plus=(1,1,1), target 0.
Independently permute prefix words

    target jump list: 1,1,1,sink
    site 1 raw stack: S,S,0,0,0,2
    site 2 raw stack: S,1,1

and append the same finite leftward jump continuations to bounds (6,8,3).
There are 720 equal-weight geometry cylinders. All necessary stabilizations
fit within the finite bounds; later stack instructions are unrestricted.
There are 404 bad marked cells and 379 good marked cells, with ranks 2,3,4.
Geometry equality here is conditional on this finite inventory class. Its
probability under the unconditional stack law can depend on parameters.

The conditional occupation difference remains

    q_plus - q_minus = p*z*(11-22*z-14*z^2)/720.

Thus the nontrivial feasible interval is p in [p_*,1), where

    p_* = (25-5*sqrt(11))/14, approximately 0.6012,
    z_* = (-11+5*sqrt(11))/14.

The degenerate endpoints p=0 and p=1 are trivial separately. A negative
conditional difference below p_* is not an unconditional counterexample.

2. THE RESTRICTED STACK REWRITES

The main search uses mode='causal' in local_weighted_search.py. A step is:

  (a) transpose two distinct cards in the four-card target jump prefix; or
  (b) transpose an outside jump card belonging to the backward ancestor set
      of a lower target terminal sleep, with another distinct card in the
      same outside prefix. The partner may be a sleep card.

After each step rebuild and restabilize the field, then compute new allowed
moves. The backward dependencies come from the actual legal stabilization
events in search.py, including arrivals and the preceding event at each site.

During exploration from source mark j, j remains the recorded obligation.
When j<=L_minus of the current geometry, use the lower terminal sleep at
chance j as the root. When j>L_minus, use its LAST available lower chance
min(j,L_minus) only to choose the exploration root. This does not change j,
and does not assert that the marked success currently exists. In this ensemble
L_minus is always at least one. Target transpositions need not have a causal
anchor. Card inventories and the common finite continuation stay fixed.

A final good partner can have rank k<=j. Intermediate types are evaluated
at the original j; the final mark k is selected at the endpoint. Every path
is a search witness between completed fields, not a chronological sequence
of physical particle operations. Individual adaptive swaps are not claimed
to preserve the iid law. The final fractional interval ledger, together with
the full-stack lift in the previous continuation, supplies exact marginals.
The inverse endpoint exchange also need not follow this directed search rule.

3. SINGLE-INPUT REPAIR DOES NOT CONTROL COLLECTIVE CAPACITY

For radius one, allow ANY final good rank, making the test less restrictive
than the eventual earlier-rank construction. At p=3/4, capacities proportional
to marked-cell masses are 16,4,1 for ranks 2,3,4. Total bad demand is 4217.
The exact maximum flow is 3926, leaving deficit 291.

The residual cut identifies 194 bad cells whose complete one-step good
neighborhood has 155 cells. Their demand is 2192 and supply is 1901.
More strongly, the same family's demand minus supply is

    (p*z/720) * (13+19*z+7*z^2),

strictly positive for every 0<p<1. Hence no fractional bad-to-good pairing
using these one-step edges can repair the entire reservoir at any such p.

Yet EVERY bad cell has a one-step good neighbor of rank no later than its
own. Each bad cell can therefore be repaired individually, for every p,
using at most the mass of that one good neighbor. At p=3/4 the smallest
singleton neighbor-capacity/source-capacity ratio is exactly one. The failure
is competition for shared good capacity, not the absence of a repair path
for a particular input.

This is the concrete reason the proof must follow families or an explicit
mass ledger. Finding a successful rewrite separately for each input is too
weak, even when its individual weight comparison is favorable.

Files: one_exchange_hall_certificate.json records the cut and full flow;
one_exchange_individual_checks.json records the singleton check.
At p=3/4, two steps with final k<=j suffice even if lower marked success is
protected at every internal state: two_exchange_protected_certificate.json.

4. EXACT THREE-STEP COUPLING AT THE CRITICAL PARAMETER

At z=z_*, use capacities 196*z_*^(j-2), j=2,3,4. In the exact basis
a+b*sqrt(11), these are

    rank 2: 196
    rank 3: -154+70*sqrt(11)
    rank 4: 396-110*sqrt(11).

quadratic_flow.py uses exact integer-pair arithmetic and sign comparisons
by squaring; no floating-point capacity decisions enter the certificate.
It restricts partners to those reachable in at most three steps under the
above rules, with k<=j and unrestricted intermediate types.

There are 39,139 allowed source/destination edges. The exact maximum flow
equals all bad demand and all good supply:

    34684+6590*sqrt(11).

The certificate has 605 positive fractional transfers. Independently replaying
their stack edits gives 32 paths of length one, 117 of length two, and 456
of length three, for 1,634 exchanges in total. The verifier checks endpoint
types, card values, allowed causal anchors, earlier final ranks, and every
incoming and outgoing exact mass budget. The preexisting independent raw

This establishes a complete coupling conditional on this reservoir at p_*.
Extending it by identity outside the reservoir is a marginal-preserving
partial repair globally; it leaves other bad fields unresolved. Three steps
is a sufficiency statement, not a claim that every alternative two-step rule
must fail.

5. A GENERAL EARLIER-RANK PARAMETER-EXTENSION LEMMA

Let geometry cells a have fixed weights mu_a, independent of the target sleep
parameter. The marked-cell mass at source rank j is

    w_a(p) = mu_a * p*z^(j-1).

Suppose at p_0 in (0,1) a fractional coupling transports every bad source
a=(Xi_a,j_a) into good cells b=(Xi_b,k_b), always with k_b<=j_a.
Let theta_ab be the FRACTION of source a sent to b. Thus sum_b theta_ab=1.
Keep these source fractions at any p>=p_0, and send theta_ab*w_a(p).
The load on destination b, divided by its available marked-cell mass, is

    Load_b(p) = sum_a theta_ab * (mu_a/mu_b) * z^(j_a-k_b).

All coefficients are nonnegative and all powers are nonnegative integers.
For z<=z_0, Load_b(p)<=Load_b(p_0)<=1. Every source is fully sent and every
destination respects its capacity. The same paths remain available because
the geometry/rank rewrite graph does not depend on p. This proves extension
to all p>=p_0 (with degenerate endpoints interpreted separately if needed).

To realize the coupling explicitly, reserve disjoint source intervals of
length theta_ab in an auxiliary uniform variable. Reserve destination
intervals of length theta_ab*w_a(p)/w_b(p). Swap each interval pair by the
affine map preserving its probability mass, with the inverse on the partner.
Fix unused good intervals and cells of types 00 and 11. This is a
measure-preserving involution, so f(a)<=g(T(a)) and both marginals are exact.
The earlier continuation explains how to lift the augmented representation
to full iid stack fields for each of the two stabilizations.

Applying the lemma to the exact critical certificate proves the three-step
repair throughout p in [p_*,1), not merely at a numerical grid of rates.
In the reservoir the conditional geometry weights are all equal for every
allowed rate. The lemma itself is a general transport fact; it is not a proof
that ARW supplies the required earlier-rank transport on arbitrary graphs.

6. THE MARK NEED NOT SURVIVE AS A REALIZED SLEEP DURING SEARCH

At the critical rate, exhaust the finite reachable graph (radius 720, which
includes every simple path on the 720 geometries). Keep the final k<=j rule.
If lower success at rank j must hold at every internal field, mode='causal'
still has a Hall-deficient family, with demand minus supply proportional to

    -11+36*z+20*z^2.

Even allowing EITHER copy to succeed at rank j at every internal field leaves
a deficient family, with polynomial

    -11+23*z+20*z^2.

At z_*, use 11=22*z_*+14*z_*^2 to obtain shortage z_*+6*z_*^2>0.
Enriching the move set to anchor in both target histories and all terminal
outside sleepers, and allowing sleep-card anchors (mode='sleep_roots'),
does not fix this protected-search obstruction. Its deficient polynomial is

    -11+22*z+18*z^2,

which equals 4*z_*^2>0 at the critical rate. The exact scaled deficits for
these three tests are respectively

    220+320*sqrt(11),
    2222-590*sqrt(11),
    1584-440*sqrt(11).

The unprotected three-step flow does succeed. Of its 605 paths, 191 have an
internal type-00 field at the original source rank. For example, one positive
transfer uses mark 3 throughout and has completed-field types

    10 -> 00 -> 00 -> 01.

Its geometry words (S=-1, sink=3) are:

    start:  [1,1,1,3]; [-1,-1,0,2,0,0]; [1,-1,1]
    step 1: [1,1,1,3]; [-1,-1,0,0,0,2]; [1,-1,1]
    step 2: [1,1,1,3]; [0,-1,0,0,-1,2]; [1,-1,1]
    step 3: [3,1,1,1]; [0,-1,0,0,-1,2]; [1,-1,1].

The first two swaps have the required outside causal anchors; the last swaps
two target directions. Its exact positive transferred mass in the scaled
units is 36-10*sqrt(11). The trace is saved in the verification summary.

Scope matters: this rules out the stated success-protection invariant for
these finite-reservoir rules with a fixed source rank during exploration.
It does NOT show that every conceivable coupling must visit a 00 field.
Changing ranks during exploration, changing the reservoir, or using other
moves could avoid this obstruction. Within this architecture the useful
object is a weighted obligation to produce a successful endpoint, not a
specific sleep event required to survive every intermediate rewrite.

File: protected_components_results.json. These are exact finite max-flow
calculations, not a conclusion from a search timeout.

7. THE NEXT GENERAL STEP

The useful architecture is now a family-based exploration with an exact
fractional ledger. Begin with a family of unmatched marked histories; expose
its backward rewrite neighborhood; allow paths through neutral fields; and
use a residual cut to identify the whole family whose demand exceeds available
good mass. Enlarge that family's move neighborhood or stack reservoir. Avoid
spending the same good interval twice. Earlier endpoint ranks are particularly
useful because the parameter-extension lemma makes their capacity robust.

What remains is an ARW-specific supply argument. A finite max-flow certificate
computes the desired transport, but cannot explain why a general graph has
enough good mass. Weighted Hall inequalities, if merely assumed for all
families, restate the essential difficulty. A promising concrete target is a
nonnegative current generated by Abelian outside-excursion commutations, with
bad mass as source, good mass as sink, and controlled reservoir-boundary terms.
An identity of this kind could prove the required family inequalities without
assuming the desired occupation comparison. Intermediate search fields can
carry paths without consuming endpoint capacity; those paths are not claimed
to preserve marginal laws at each internal step.

The unproved pieces are a uniform local current or expansion rule, control
of infinite exploration and boundary terms, and a limiting construction
that preserves the exact marginal laws. The present finite coupling resolves
none of those by itself. Its contribution is to distinguish invariants that
actually help (exact mass, earlier endpoint ranks) from invariants that can
block a feasible repair (one-step repairs and protected realized sleeps).

8. REPRODUCTION AND FILE GUIDE

Run in this directory with Python 3, NumPy and SciPy installed:

    python verify_local_weighted.py

This replays the saved critical certificate and rewrites its verification
summary. To recompute the exact critical max flow:

    python quadratic_flow.py

local_weighted_search.py defines the allowed moves, breadth-first exploration,
and integer max-flow tests. Its default main is exploratory and writes
local_weighted_results.json and local_weighted_certificate.json; these are
not the primary critical certificate. Historical parameter sweeps are in
local_weighted_parameter_results.json, three_exchange_results.json,
protected_sleep_results.json, and outside_roots_results.json. Weight scales
in older result files can differ by a common factor; compare normalized
masses, or use each file's recorded weights. The earlier continuation and
its scripts remain intact.
