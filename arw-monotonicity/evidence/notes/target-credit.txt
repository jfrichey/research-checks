1. EXACT REPRESENTATION AND SINGLE-DEBT CONDITION

For the signed-renewal response at common-particle bound two, DF's identity
and first-collision resummation give

 Delta = C + sum_y d_y G_y,

where G_y is target-hit probability from two active particles at y. C is
direct target credit before the first collision, including the uniform
defect representation's constant. C and d retain the creation law through
nu; they are not fitted using G. General signed-renewal invertibility is
still unproved. The failed case's matrix identities are checked exactly.

For one negative coefficient d_z=-b and C>=0, stop each positive-source
trajectory at collision C_z, target hit, or first sleep/sink loss. Write m
for weighted meeting probability and a for weighted successful target hits.
Then

 Delta = C+a+(m-b)G_z + nonnegative continuation credits.

Thus C+a+m>=b suffices, as MC established. total_credit.py computes C,d
directly from one-particle killed Green functions, without computing G,
and uses policy iteration to maximize P(C_z or target before sleep/loss).
Every causal policy is proper: an effective jump from a collision either
leaves that collision or is a self-loop; accessibility eventually permits
separation/loss, and at distinct sites the positive minimum sleep rate
bounds survival. Standard finite-state reachability policy optimization
therefore applies. Bellman residuals are numerical diagnostics only.

4. GENERAL HITTING CEILING (PROOF; NO NOVELTY CLAIM)

Let h(x) be ordinary jump-chain probability of hitting target v before the
sink, with h(v)=1, h(sink)=0 and h=Kh elsewhere. For particle positions
x_1,...,x_m, including sleepers, define

 F(x_1,...,x_m)=1-product_i(1-h(x_i)).

A sink particle can equivalently remain with factor 1. Sleep instructions
do not change F. At a jump of a chosen particle away from v, harmonicity
in that one coordinate preserves expected F; waking changes only activity
labels. Thus F stopped at the first target hit is a bounded martingale for
every causal legal schedule. On a hit it equals 1 and is always nonnegative.
Finite-time optional stopping and a limit give P(ever hit v)<=F(initial).
This proof does not assume independence of stopped particle trajectories.

For two particles at y,

 0 <= G_y <= u_y := 2h(y)-h(y)^2 <= 1.

The resulting sufficient MULTIPLE-debt condition is

 C >= sum_y max(-d_y,0) u_y.                    (A)

It discards positive sources, so it remains stronger than occupation
monotonicity. The exact Section 3 example satisfies (A) because the two
debt ceilings vanish. By continuity it also holds for all sufficiently
small positive epsilon in that example. No general validity is claimed.

For ONE debt, the stopping decomposition also gives the sharper sufficient
condition C>=0 and

 C+a+m u_z >= b u_z.                           (B)

If m>=b, positivity is immediate. Otherwise use G_z<=u_z. When u_z=1,
(B) is MC's original condition. This shows exactly where a target-aware
ceiling enters, without using previously computed G values.
